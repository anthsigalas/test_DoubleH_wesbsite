Our service

Flexible funding

Many churches are unable to secure sufficient funding through traditional lending alone.

Double Honour provides flexible solutions to help bridge that gap, enabling churches to purchase property sooner while building a sustainable pathway to ownership.

Every solution is designed to be sustainable, appropriate and beneficial for the church in the long term.

Flexible funding is only one part of a successful property purchase.
