Our name

Double Honour?

Let the elders who rule well be considered worthy of double honour, especially those who labour in preaching and teaching.”

— 1 Timothy 5:17

The name Double Honour comes from the Greek phrase “διπλῆς τιμῆς” (diplēs timēs)
in 1 Timothy 5:17.

In the following verse, Paul grounds this honour in practical provision: “the labourer deserves his wages”. Honour is therefore not only about respect and appreciation, but also about materially supporting those who labour in the Word.

Yet many ministers serve on modest stipends while facing the same pressures of housing, daily life, and raising a family as everyone else. We believe helping a church provide a secure home for its minister is one of the most effective ways of giving that honour practical substance.

A secure home can relieve a significant burden on ministers and their families, help them remain close to the communities they serve, and enable them to fully focus on prayer, preaching, teaching and pastoral care. The benefit reaches beyond one household:
when ministers are able to serve sustainably, the whole church is strengthened.

Our mission

Build
lasting assets
for ministry

We believe churches should invest in long-term property assets rather than rely on temporary solutions, especially when the denominational support is at risk.

We help churches overcome common barriers to property ownership by bridging funding gaps and providing tailored funding solutions where traditional mortgage options may be limited.

Our mission is to simplify the property acquisition process so that ministers can focus on their ministry while building a lasting foundation for future generations.
